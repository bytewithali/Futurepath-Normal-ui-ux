      Frequently asked questions

      Do I need prior experience?

      No. You can start as a complete beginner. Onboarding asks your level and sets where your roadmap begins.

      How much time do I need each day?

      You choose 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours or 3+ hours. Lessons are sized to match.

      Why can’t I skip to the next module?

      Skills build on each other. A reviewed project proves you can apply a skill before the next one unlocks.

      What if my project needs improvement?

      You receive written feedback, fix the issues and resubmit as many times as needed.

      Will the AI mentor do my assignments?

      No. FuturePath AI guides you with explanations, hints and resources, so the work stays yours.
